Scientific Rigor (C-04)

Wald GMICE, ShakeMap v4 & GEM Fragility

Ground Motion: GMICE Fallback & ShakeMap v4

C-04 Compliant

Fallback empirical GMICE (Wald et al., 1999; California MMI V–VIII calibration, auto-flagged when extrapolating):

PGA = 10^((MMI + 1.66) / 3.66) / 980.665

Production Upgrade: Ingest station-constrained ShakeMap v4 PGA, PGV, and Vs30 (NEHRP Site Classes A–E) directly via drr_fdsn_shakemap_fetch.

Damage State Exceedance: Lognormal CDF

GEM / HAZUS

Conditional probability of structural damage state exceedance given intensity measure IM:

P(DS ≥ ds_i | IM) = Φ( (ln(IM / θ_i)) / β_i )

Mean Damage Ratio: MDR = ∑ P(DS = ds_i) × DF(ds_i), paired with analytical bounds & seeded Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation.
