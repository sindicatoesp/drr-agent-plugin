    Built for Operational EOCs
    
            
    Scientific rigor without cloud lock-in or bloated dependencies.
    
          
    
          
    
            Designed for national disaster management offices, UN duty stations, and air-gapped emergency operations centers where auditability and offline resilience are non-negotiable.
          
    
        
    

        
    
          
    
            
    01 / AUTO-ENRICHMENT
    
            
    Zero-Gap Exposure Fallbacks
    
            
    
              When a YELLOW, ORANGE, or RED event strikes outside your local GIS layer, the monitor automatically queries OpenStreetMap critical infrastructure across 9 Sendai Target D sectors and discovers public OCHA HDX COD-AB & COD-PS datasets with zero API keys.
            
    
            
    OSM Overpass · HDX CKAN v3 · Static Packs
    
          
    

          
    
            
    02 / SCIENTIFIC GOVERNANCE
    
            
    Honest Uncertainty & Sendai Rules
    
            
    
              Missing data is preserved as null (unknown ≠ 0). Modelled population exposure and displacement are kept strictly distinct from Sendai Target B-1 directly affected people, and physical facility damage (D-2..D-4) is separated from lifeline disruption (D-6..D-8).
            
    
            
    Sendai A–D · UNDRR HIPs · Replay Harness
    
          
    

          
    
            
    03 / FAIL-CLOSED ALERTING
    
            
    Governed CAP v1.2 & DELTA Sync
    
            
    
              CAP XML alerts default to Draft status, read-only shadow mode blocks consequential external writes, SSRF guards protect webhooks and custom endpoints, and country scoping prevents foreign events from polluting national UNDRR DELTA Resilience instances.
            
    
            
    OASIS CAP 1.2 · DELTA Guard · Air-Gap Mode
