Scientific Rigor

Wald GMPE & GEM Fragility Formulation

Ground Motion: MMI to PGA ($g$)

Automated conversion of Instrumental Modified Mercalli Intensity to Peak Ground Acceleration via Wald et al. (1999):

PGA = 10^((MMI + 1.66) / 3.66) / 980.665

Ensures seismic telemetry immediately maps into engineering acceleration units required for structural vulnerability.

Damage State Exceedance: Lognormal CDF

Conditional probability of structural damage state exceedance given intensity measure $IM$:

P(DS ≥ ds_i | IM) = Φ( (ln(IM / θ_i)) / β_i )

Mean Damage Ratio: MDR = ∑ P(DS = ds_i) × DF(ds_i), directly yielding monetary loss percentages.
